Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Tiddwell, LLC, a Washington limited liability company ("Tiddwell," "we," "us," or "our"), provides Tiddwell, a locally installed accounting application that operates as a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server for use with Claude Desktop. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with the Tiddwell application and our website at www.tiddwell.com (together, the "Services").

By downloading and using Tiddwell, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

1. How Tiddwell Works

Tiddwell is installed and runs entirely on your local machine. All accounting data you create through Tiddwell — including company information, transactions, vendors, accounts, and financial reports — is stored in a local database file on your computer. Tiddwell does not transmit your accounting data to Tiddwell, LLC or any third-party server.

Your conversations with Claude Desktop are governed by Anthropic's privacy policy, not this one. Tiddwell, LLC does not have access to your conversations with Claude.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Because Tiddwell operates locally on your machine, we do not collect, receive, store, or have access to:

  • Your accounting data, financial records, or transaction history
  • Company, vendor, or customer information you enter into Tiddwell
  • Your conversations with Claude Desktop
  • Usage analytics or telemetry from the application
  • Account credentials (Tiddwell does not require an account)
  • Payment information (Tiddwell is free)

3. Information We May Receive

A. Support Communications

If you contact us at support@tiddwell.com, we will receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message, such as error descriptions or log file contents. We use this information only to respond to your inquiry and improve the Services.

B. Website Information

When you visit www.tiddwell.com, our website hosting provider may automatically collect standard web server information such as IP address, browser type, and pages viewed. We do not use cookies or tracking tools on our website for advertising or behavioral targeting.

C. Download Information

If you download Tiddwell from a platform such as GitHub, that platform may collect information about the download according to its own privacy policy. Tiddwell, LLC does not independently track who downloads the application.

4. How We Use Information

Any information we do receive is used only to:

  • Respond to support inquiries
  • Maintain and improve the Services
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade any information. We do not use any information for advertising or cross-context behavioral targeting. We do not use any information to train or improve artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

5. Data You Control

Your accounting data is stored locally at %LocalAppData%\Tiddwell\tiddwell.db on your Windows machine. You have full control over this file. You can back it up, move it, or delete it at any time. Uninstalling Tiddwell does not automatically delete your data file.

Tiddwell, LLC has no ability to access, recover, or restore your local data. You are solely responsible for backing up your data.

6. Third-Party Services

Tiddwell is designed to work with Claude Desktop, which is provided by Anthropic, PBC. Your use of Claude Desktop is governed by Anthropic's terms of service and privacy policy. Tiddwell, LLC is not affiliated with Anthropic and has no access to or control over your Claude Desktop account or conversations.

7. Security

Because your data is stored locally on your machine, the security of your accounting data depends on your own device security practices, including keeping your operating system updated, using strong passwords, and maintaining appropriate access controls on your computer.

8. Children's Privacy

The Services are intended only for individuals 18 years of age or older, consistent with our Terms of Service. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may modify this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates are effective when posted. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when this Privacy Policy was last revised.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@tiddwell.com, or Tiddwell, LLC, 522 W Riverside Ave, Spokane WA 99201.
